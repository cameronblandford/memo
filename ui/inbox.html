Keys: j and k select a memo. In the one-card view, the left and right arrows (or j and k) go to the previous and the next card, and a swipe does the same; nothing changes until you use a button. o opens or closes the Details (the full text) of the selected memo. + and - change the impact. w shows the work memos or all memos. Command-Return sends the reply. In the capture field, Return sends and Shift-Return adds a line. To attach an image, use the picture icon in the field, paste, or drop the file.
